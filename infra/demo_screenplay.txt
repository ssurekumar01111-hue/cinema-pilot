                              STATIC

                    An Original Short Screenplay
               For CinemaPilot Internal Demo Use Only
              Not Based on Any Existing Film or Property

                          Written: August 2026

================================================================================

CHARACTERS

  DR. NADIA VOSS  — 43, astrophysicist. Tenacious, perpetually caffeinated.
  FELIX CRANE     — 26, Nadia's grad student. Anxious, sharper than he looks.
  AGENT MARGOT REYES — 35, government operative. Calm, precise, unreadable.
  EDGAR PARK      — 56, Nadia's former mentor. White-haired, conflicted, late.

LOCATIONS

  Mesa Verde Radio Observatory  — remote plateau, three giant satellite dishes
  University Astrophysics Lab   — fluorescent lights, whiteboards, burnt coffee
  University Campus Parking Lot — sodium lamps, black SUV
  Government SUV                — moving, tinted windows
  Millbrook Storage Unit        — ten by twenty feet, bare bulb  [DEMO SCENE 5]

PROPS (named)

  Crumpled frequency printout, THERMOS (Nadia's), battered TRANSISTOR RADIO,
  RED USB DRIVE, laptop bag, burner phone, empty chip bag, broken office chair,
  rolled-up rug, motivational poster ("HANG IN THERE" — kitten on a branch),
  printed image of the observatory.

FADE IN:


==============================================================================
SCENE 1 — WONDER / QUIET TENSION
==============================================================================

EXT. MESA VERDE RADIO OBSERVATORY — NIGHT

A vast plateau. Ink-black sky, too many stars. Three enormous satellite dishes
tilt toward the heavens like supplicants. A battered PICKUP TRUCK is parked
alone beside a small equipment shed.

DR. NADIA VOSS (43, tangled hair, coffee-stained field jacket) leans against
the truck's hood, staring at a LAPTOP balanced on a stack of frequency
printouts. The silence is absolute.

A BEEP from the laptop. Then another. Then a cascade.

Nadia sets down her THERMOS and leans in close.

                              NADIA
                    (barely audible)
                    That's not noise.

She tears off a fresh sheet from the portable printer — a CRUMPLED FREQUENCY
PRINTOUT — and holds it up against the starlight, squinting.

                              NADIA (CONT'D)
                    Felix. Felix, wake up.

She raps on the truck's rear window. FELIX CRANE (26, beanie, perpetually
underdressed for the altitude) startles awake in the truck bed, knocking over
an EMPTY CHIP BAG.

                              FELIX
                    (groggy)
                    Is it the coyotes again?

                              NADIA
                    It's not coyotes.

She holds up the printout. Felix squints at it. His eyes widen.

                              FELIX
                    That's — that's structured.

                              NADIA
                    Fifty-eight-point-three gigahertz.
                    Repeating every four minutes and
                    seventeen seconds. For the past
                    two hours.

                              FELIX
                    From where?

Nadia turns the laptop toward him. A single point on a star map pulses red.

                              NADIA
                    Sector 12-Bravo. Deep field. Nothing's
                    there, Felix. Nothing should be there.

A long silence. Wind moves through the dish cables — a low, resonant hum,
almost like a held note.

                              FELIX
                              (quiet)
                    What does it sound like?

Nadia pulls a battered TRANSISTOR RADIO from the equipment shed — old,
wired to the laptop with a tangle of adapters — and turns the dial. A pattern
rises from static. Rhythmic. Almost musical.

They both go still and listen.

                              FELIX (CONT'D)
                    It sounds like counting.


                                                              CUT TO:


==============================================================================
SCENE 2 — URGENT / THRILLER
==============================================================================

INT. UNIVERSITY ASTROPHYSICS LAB — NIGHT

Fluorescent lights hum overhead. Whiteboards dense with equations. The smell
of burnt coffee and dry-erase marker. It is very late and very bright.

Nadia and Felix are at their stations, wired in, monitors lit. The FREQUENCY
PRINTOUT is pinned to the wall, annotated in red marker. A hand-drawn waveform
diagram covers half a whiteboard.

                              FELIX
                    Okay. It's not random. I ran it
                    through three pattern models.
                    Look.

He spins his monitor toward her. Columns of numbers, color-coded.

                              NADIA
                    Base-twelve counting. Ascending
                    from... twelve, up to —

                              FELIX
                              (finishing)
                    — one. It's a countdown.

A beat. The fluorescent lights hum.

                              NADIA
                    How long until it hits one?

                              FELIX
                    At current interval... fourteen hours,
                    twenty-two minutes.

                              NADIA
                    And then?

                              FELIX
                    I have absolutely no idea.

Nadia stands and paces. She picks up her THERMOS — empty — and sets it
down with a hollow clank. She paces some more.

                              NADIA
                    Who else knows about this?

                              FELIX
                    Just us. I haven't logged it yet.
                    I was about to —

                              NADIA
                    Don't. Not yet. Twelve hours.

                              FELIX
                    Nadia, if this is real —

                              NADIA
                    If this is real, the moment we
                    file, we lose it. Government
                    protocols. Clearance reviews.
                    Security holds. It gets buried
                    in a committee room and we
                    never see it again.

Felix chews his lip. He makes a decision: pulls a RED USB DRIVE from his
jacket pocket and plugs it in. Data begins to copy.

                              FELIX
                    Twelve hours.

                              NADIA
                              (almost a smile)
                    Twelve hours.

Felix's PHONE buzzes face-up on the desk. He glances at it. His expression
changes.

                              FELIX
                    There's someone in the parking lot.

They both go very still.


                                                              CUT TO:


==============================================================================
SCENE 3 — ACTION / CONFRONTATION
==============================================================================

EXT. UNIVERSITY CAMPUS — PARKING LOT — NIGHT

A black SUV idles under a sodium lamp. AGENT MARGOT REYES (35, sharp suit,
sharper eyes) stands outside it, phone to her ear, utterly unhurried.

                              REYES
                              (into phone)
                    Confirmation. Signal flagged on
                    the federal passive array forty
                    minutes ago. Voss's lab is dark
                    but her card swiped at 11:52.

She listens.

                              REYES (CONT'D)
                    Yes, sir. Retrieval only. No
                    escalation unless she runs.

A light flicks off in a second-floor window. Reyes sees it.

                              REYES (CONT'D)
                    She's running.

She signals to two FIGURES by the SUV without raising her voice. They move
toward the building entrance, calm and quick.

From a fire exit at the far end of the building: Nadia bursts out at a dead
sprint. She clutches a LAPTOP BAG and — absurdly, out of pure reflex — her
THERMOS.

Reyes watches her cut across the parking lot, weaving between cars.

                              REYES (CONT'D)
                              (into phone)
                    She's running.

A pause.

                              REYES (CONT'D)
                              (to the driver)
                    Cut her off at Millbrook. Gently.

She doesn't run. She walks, calmly, toward the SUV, and opens the rear door
herself.


                                                              CUT TO:


==============================================================================
SCENE 4 — TENSE DIALOGUE / SURPRISE REVEAL
==============================================================================

INT. GOVERNMENT SUV — MOVING — NIGHT

Nadia sits in the back seat. LAPTOP BAG beside her. She has not let go of the
THERMOS. Reyes sits opposite, studying her.

                              NADIA
                    You're going to tell me this is
                    for national security.

                              REYES
                    I'm going to tell you we're having
                    a conversation. That's all.

                              NADIA
                    Then I can leave.

                              REYES
                    In about twenty minutes, sure.

The city slides past the tinted windows. Traffic. Ordinary life.

                              REYES (CONT'D)
                    We've been monitoring that frequency
                    band for three years, Dr. Voss.
                    You had the right equipment in the
                    right place on the right night.
                    That's remarkable. But you're not
                    the first person to find it.

                              NADIA
                              (quietly)
                    Who was?

The partition between front and back seats slides open. In the passenger
seat sits EDGAR PARK (56, white-haired, reading glasses on a chain around
his neck). He turns to look at Nadia. His expression is one of genuine regret.

                              NADIA (CONT'D)
                    Edgar.

                              EDGAR
                              (gently)
                    Hello, Nadia.

A long, wounded silence. The SUV makes a smooth turn.

                              NADIA
                    You've known.

                              EDGAR
                    Two years. I'm sorry.

                              NADIA
                              (controlled fury)
                    You knew and you didn't tell me.
                    You sat in my seminar. You reviewed
                    my grant applications —

                              EDGAR
                    I was protecting you. If you'd
                    found it independently, under
                    official aegis, there would have
                    been protection. A framework. Now—

                              NADIA
                    Now I'm in a car.

                              REYES
                    Now you're in a car. And your grad
                    student has a red USB drive in his
                    jacket pocket.

Nadia's expression does not change. Which tells Reyes everything.

                              REYES (CONT'D)
                    We'll need that back.


                                                              CUT TO:


==============================================================================
SCENE 5 — QUIET / COMEDIC RELIEF
[RELOCATION DEMO SCENE — see production note below]
==============================================================================

INT. MILLBROOK STORAGE UNIT — NIGHT

  PRODUCTION NOTE: This scene is written for a plain interior for budget
  reasons. The action (phone call, isolation, character beat) is location-
  agnostic. For the relocation demo, this scene can be moved to: an outdoor
  park bench, a beach at night, a rooftop, a marina dock, a street corner,
  etc. Nothing in the scene's dialogue or action depends on being indoors.

A corrugated-metal storage unit, ten by twenty feet. A single bare bulb
swings gently overhead. Cardboard boxes, a broken OFFICE CHAIR, a rolled-up
RUG. The kind of place that smells like old decisions.

FELIX sits on a box marked FRAGILE. He still has the RED USB DRIVE clipped
to his jacket zipper. He has his phone. He looks like a person who did not
plan to be here.

His phone buzzes. UNKNOWN NUMBER. He stares at it. Answers.

                              FELIX
                              (hushed)
                    Hello?

                              NADIA (V.O.)
                              (barely audible, through static)
                    It's me. Burner. Don't say my name.

                              FELIX
                    Are you okay?

                              NADIA (V.O.)
                    Physically, yes. Professionally,
                    debatable. Do you still have it?

Felix touches the USB drive on his zipper.

                              FELIX
                    Yeah.

                              NADIA (V.O.)
                    They know. Don't go back to the
                    lab, don't go home, don't —

                              FELIX
                              (whispering louder than intended)
                    I'm in a storage unit, Nadia. I have
                    eleven granola bars and no blanket.
                    My "don't go home" situation is
                    already fully operational.

A pause on the line.

                              NADIA (V.O.)
                              (suppressing something)
                    Eleven granola bars.

                              FELIX
                    I panicked and grabbed the emergency
                    bag. It's mostly granola bars and
                    a phone charger.

                              NADIA (V.O.)
                    That's actually... not bad.

                              FELIX
                    There's also a motivational poster
                    from 2019. "HANG IN THERE."

                              NADIA (V.O.)
                    Felix.

                              FELIX
                    Right. Sorry. Focusing.

                              NADIA (V.O.)
                    The countdown. Fourteen hours from
                    when we left the lab — now about
                    twelve. Whatever is at the end of
                    it, we need to decode the final
                    frame before it zeros out. The
                    data is on that drive.

                              FELIX
                    And you need me to run it.

                              NADIA (V.O.)
                    I need you to run it. Find somewhere
                    with power and a connection. Not
                    your accounts — use the observatory
                    guest credentials. I changed them
                    last Tuesday.

                              FELIX
                    Observatory guest credentials.
                    Got it.

                              NADIA (V.O.)
                    And Felix?

                              FELIX
                    Yeah?

                              NADIA (V.O.)
                    Thank you. For grabbing it. I know
                    you were scared.

A long beat.

                              FELIX
                              (quietly)
                    I'm still scared.

                              NADIA (V.O.)
                    Good. Scared people pay attention.

The call ends. Felix stares at the USB drive. He looks up at the bare bulb.

                              FELIX
                              (to himself)
                    Okay.

He stands. Slings his bag. Pauses at the door — and looks back at the
motivational poster. A stock photo of a kitten dangling from a branch.

                              FELIX (CONT'D)
                    Yeah. Fair enough.

He slips out into the dark.


                                                              CUT TO:


==============================================================================
SCENE 6 — CLIMAX / EMOTIONAL
==============================================================================

INT. UNIVERSITY ASTROPHYSICS LAB — DAY

Early morning light through tall windows. The lab has been searched: drawers
open, monitors dark, cables unplugged. Someone has been thorough.

But Felix is here. He's in the corner by a WALL SOCKET, laptop open and
running. He's been here since 4 AM. He looks like it.

The door opens. Nadia walks in.

                              FELIX
                    How did you —

                              NADIA
                    Edgar. He left the back-door
                    keypad code in a voicemail from
                    2024 that I never deleted.
                    He's old-fashioned.

She pulls up a chair beside him. Looks at the screen without sitting down
first. Then sits.

                              FELIX
                    Three hours left. I've been running
                    the final-frame decode since 4 AM.
                    It's not what I thought it would be.

                              NADIA
                    What do you mean?

Felix turns the screen toward her.

                              FELIX
                    It's not a countdown to something
                    happening. It's a countdown to
                    something being revealed. The last
                    data block is a compressed image
                    file. It's been transmitting in
                    fragments across the entire sequence.
                    We've been receiving it for two hours
                    and didn't know.

Nadia stares. Her hand finds the THERMOS — still the same one, impossibly
still full somehow — and she takes a slow sip without looking away from
the screen.

                              NADIA
                    What's the image?

                              FELIX
                    Still decoding. But look at the
                    coordinate header on the file.

She looks. A long silence. She sets the thermos down.

                              NADIA
                    That's not a location in space.

                              FELIX
                    No.

                              NADIA
                    Those are Earth coordinates.

                              FELIX
                              (barely above a whisper)
                    Pacific coast. Forty-seven degrees
                    north.

They look at each other.

The laptop CHIMES softly. The image resolves: sharp, photographic, detailed.


                                                              CUT TO:


==============================================================================
SCENE 7 — QUIET / HOPEFUL / RESOLUTION
==============================================================================

EXT. MESA VERDE RADIO OBSERVATORY — DAWN

First light. The plateau glows pale gold. The great dishes are motionless
against a sky that is changing from black to blue to something in between.

Nadia stands alone outside the equipment shed. She holds a PRINTOUT — the
decoded image. It is a photograph-quality rendering of this observatory.
These dishes. This exact place. Transmitted from somewhere very far away.

As if whoever sent the signal already knew they'd be standing here. As if
they were sending something back.

Footsteps in the gravel. Edgar approaches, coat rumpled, looking his age.

                              EDGAR
                    Agent Reyes will be here within
                    the hour. We don't have long to
                    decide what we —

                              NADIA
                    She can have it.

Edgar blinks. That was not what he expected.

                              NADIA (CONT'D)
                    All of it. Raw files, full decode,
                    every log. We document that we
                    found it first, we retain academic
                    credit — in writing, Edgar, signed
                    tonight — and then we hand it over.

                              EDGAR
                    Why?

Nadia holds up the printout. She doesn't look at him when she says it.

                              NADIA
                    Because this isn't ours to keep.
                    Whatever this is — whoever sent it —
                    they chose this place. They'll make
                    contact again. And when they do,
                    I want to be standing here.

She looks out at the dishes.

                              NADIA (CONT'D)
                    Not in a government car. Here.

A long silence. Wind through the cables. That same low, resonant hum.

Edgar looks at the printout. Then at the sky. Something in his face loosens.

                              EDGAR
                              (quietly)
                    You were always better at this
                    than me.

                              NADIA
                    I know.

She almost smiles.

Felix emerges from the equipment shed, thermos in hand — he must have
refilled it at some point — and hands it to Nadia without a word.

She takes it. They stand there, the three of them, watching the dishes
catch the morning light.

The wind moves through the cables.

The low hum continues.


                                                          FADE TO BLACK.

                              THE END
